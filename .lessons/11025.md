## Context

`AppLayout.tsx`'s main content row is a flex item wrapping the whole panel grid; `TwoPaneSplitLayout.tsx` wraps each half of a split terminal pane in its own flex item with an implicit `auto` row track. PR #10694 (fixing #10693, "assistant pane show/hide orphans status-line rows via PTY resize storm") changed that row's `overflow` from `hidden` to `clip` because `hidden` makes the element a scroll container, and a scroll container lets focus-driven `scrollIntoView` calls (from the HelpPanel's off-canvas xterm focusing itself) shift the whole sidebar/main content sideways. That fix shipped clean and sat latent for two months because every panel kind in the grid at the time (terminals, browser panels) has no real intrinsic content height.

## Problem

Issue #11024: opening a file taller than its file-viewer panel destroyed the entire panel grid layout — instead of the pane scrolling internally, the pane (and the row around it) inflated to the file's full height and wrecked everything else on screen. The pane body already had the textbook fix in place (`flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-auto` on `file-pane-body`), which made the bug look impossible from that side and pointed investigation at `CodeViewer`'s `[&_.cm-scroller]:!overflow-visible` (CodeMirror hands scroll ownership to its outer wrapper) rather than at the ancestor chain above it.

## Root cause

`overflow: hidden` and `overflow: clip` look interchangeable (both clip content, neither shows a scrollbar) but differ in one load-bearing way: `hidden` establishes a scroll container, and per the flexbox spec a scroll container's automatic minimum size (`min-height: auto`) resolves to `0` instead of to its content's intrinsic size. `clip` does not establish a scroll container, so that automatic-minimum zeroing never kicks in — the flex item's `min-height: auto` reverts to sizing against content, and any descendant with real intrinsic height (CodeMirror's `.cm-scroller`, freed of its own overflow by the `!overflow-visible` override) pushes the row to match it. Measured live: the row's `clientHeight` was 7446px against a 1662px window. The file viewer panel was the first panel kind in the grid with actual unbounded intrinsic content height, which is why terminals and browser panels never surfaced this in two months of #10694 being live — the regression needed a specific kind of content to manifest, not a specific interaction. The same hole existed a second place: `TwoPaneSplitLayout.tsx`'s per-half wrapper `<div className="relative min-w-0">` relies on the parent's implicit `auto` grid/flex row track, which has the identical automatic-minimum-size problem independent of the `hidden`→`clip` change.

## Lesson

- **`overflow:hidden` and `overflow:clip` are not interchangeable for flex/grid sizing** even though both visually clip — only `hidden` (and `scroll`/`auto`) establishes a scroll container that zeroes the flex/grid automatic minimum size; `clip` does not. Swapping one for the other for any reason (focus-scroll suppression, paint perf) is a layout-sizing change, not just a clipping change.
- **Every `overflow-clip` flex/grid item needs an explicit `min-height: 0` (or `min-width: 0`) alongside it**, stated as a rule, not case-by-case — don't rely on the container "happening" to have no intrinsic-height children, because the next panel kind added to that grid is the one that finds the hole.
- **A latent layout bug can hide for months if no current content exercises the trap** — the absence of a symptom across many panel kinds is not evidence the fix is safe; check the actual mechanism (scroll-container semantics), not just "nothing has broken yet."
- **When a scroll-owning child still overflows its parent, check the ancestor chain's `min-height` resolution before re-auditing the child's own overflow rules** — the pane body here (`file-pane-body`, `flex-1 min-h-0 overflow-auto`) was already correct, and `CodeViewer`'s scroll-delegation to its wrapper was a red herring; the fix was three ancestor levels up in `AppLayout.tsx`.
- Diagnose by measuring, not guessing: dumping DOM `clientHeight`/`scrollHeight` up the ancestor chain in the running app is what surfaced the 7446px-vs-1662px mismatch and pinpointed the exact row.

## Applies when

- Changing `overflow` on any flex or grid container in `AppLayout.tsx`, `TwoPaneSplitLayout.tsx`, or similar row/column wrappers — verify `min-height`/`min-width` is explicit, not relied-upon-implicit, before and after the change.
- Adding a new panel kind to the grid (`src/panels/<kind>/`) that can have real intrinsic content height (documents, images, anything not terminal/browser-shaped) — test opening oversized content and confirm the pane scrolls internally instead of inflating.
- Diagnosing "a pane/panel/row is growing past its container and wrecking the layout around it" — check ancestor `overflow` values for `clip` vs `hidden`/`scroll`/`auto` and whether `min-height:0`/`min-width:0` is explicit at each flex/grid level, before assuming the leaf component's own scroll setup is broken.
- Reviewing any PR that swaps `overflow: hidden` for `overflow: clip` (or vice versa) for a UX reason like focus-scroll suppression — that trade also changes flex/grid automatic-minimum-size behavior and needs its own sizing verification, not just a check of the original symptom it was meant to fix.
