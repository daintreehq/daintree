## Context

The seven built-in light themes passed every contrast audit yet read "ugly, unbalanced, and depressing" next to the dark themes (#9711). Earlier engine work had already added the light-theme depth-budget gates (`auditSurfaceRamp`, `auditBorderSeparation`, `getLightThemeMatrixWarnings` in `shared/theme/oklch.ts` / `contrast.ts`) — the values passed the math and still looked bad. #9727 rebuilt Bondi Beach as the gold-standard light theme over seven review rounds; the same day #9732, #9734, #9735, #9737, #9738, and #9739 re-derived Hokkaido, Svalbard, Table Mountain, Serengeti, Atacama, and Bali from the template: same token/extension keys, same structural decisions, literally the same L ramp (0.882→1.0), only hue/chroma re-derived per identity.

## Problem

Symptoms were consistent across all seven themes: the workbench read as indeterminate cold gray rather than "light <hue>", the five depth surfaces compressed into one sheet, the worktree sidebar collapsed flat, and selected rows were invisible or read as pressed/disabled. The misleading part: since the themes passed WCAG and ramp audits, the tempting fix was "more contrast" — but every failure was structural/perceptual, and the audits only bound the floors, they don't produce a design.

## Root cause

Four independent mechanisms, each measurable. (1) **Chroma decay**: letting OKLCH chroma decay toward zero as surfaces approach white makes the field read as gray, not light-colored — the fix is holding chroma on ONE hue family across the whole ramp (merged Bondi: warm sand H≈88-97; Hokkaido: lavender H≈292-298). The hue must unify field, chrome, AND the invisible ladders: Bondi's rounds 1-6 ran a blue field against warm sand chrome and read as "two themes stitched together", and the temperature seam lived in the overlay/scrim/shadow washes as much as the surfaces — a cool `overlayTint` composites as grime on a warm field (merged: warm ink `#322E26`). (2) **Depth budget**: light ramps live in L≈0.88-1.0, so adjacent steps are ~0.02 L — at the perceptual floor, unable to carry figure-ground alone; the carriers are 1px borders darkened a step, two-layer shadows (contact + spread — a single soft penumbra reads as a halo with no edge), and a grid a full register deeper than the sidebar so chrome/field/well read as three planes. (3) **Elevation direction**: on a light container a darker fill reads recessed/disabled, so ALL content lifts toward white — selection elevates to `#FFFFFF` (`sidebar-active-bg`, `settings-nav-active-bg`, `worktree-quick-state-active-bg`, `dock-item-bg-active`), inputs are raised not recessed (`surface-input` overrides the engine's recessed derivation), and translucent dark washes (`overlay-soft` at 3-5%) are sub-threshold over near-white and composited selected palette rows into a dead gray slab — those rows moved to the opaque, field-hue-inked `overlay-raised`. (4) **The narrative trap**: the PR body and round-3 comment describe the blue-field composition and even declare it "the gold-standard"; the merged r7 replaced the blue field entirely. The authoritative record is the merged file's inline comments plus the gates — not the PR discussion.

## Lesson

- **Hold chroma as lightness rises — chroma decaying to zero reads as indeterminate gray, not "light".** Pick one hue family and carry it through surfaces, chrome, and every invisible ladder (`overlayTint`, scrims, shadows, `overlay-raised`); a second temperature anywhere composites as grime or a stitched seam.
- **On light themes, everything that carries content lifts TOWARD white — selection included.** Never a darker fill on a light container: selection = elevate to white (plus one restrained marker), inputs raised not recessed, hover monotonic idle < hover < selected. The light matrix (`getLightThemeMatrixWarnings`) hard-fails elevation-direction inversions.
- **~0.02-L surface steps cannot carry figure-ground; borders and shadows must.** The gates in `shared/theme/oklch.ts` (`LIGHT_RAMP_SPAN_FLOOR = 0.075`, `LIGHT_RAMP_STEP_FLOOR = 0.018`, panel→elevated must not be the smallest step) are hard-fail for light, warn-only for dark — and `builtInThemes.test.ts` has an explicit guard that the light matrix never trips a dark theme; keep new gates polarity-scoped the same way.
- **Verify against rendered pixels, not token values.** Overlay/wash tokens composite over other layers; a token that looks right in isolation dies on the real surface. `e2e/screenshots/theme-review.spec.ts` and `gh workflow run screenshots.yml -f theme=<id>` (both added by #9727, both still present) capture any theme's full chrome set.
- **Agent brand icons are recoloured, never plated (`resolveBrandMarkInk`, `src/lib/brandIcon.ts`):** a tile behind the glyph renders as a murky chip on light fields and as noise in a row — #11903 removed it for good. The mark rests at the theme's own ink lightness carrying the brand hue at one constant OKLCH chroma, and its hover colour is the brand colour with the minimum lightness correction that clears 3:1; both polarities go through the same path.
- **Don't learn this template from PR #9727's body or comments** — they document rounds 1-3 (rejected blue field). The merged `bondi.ts` r7 comments are the spec; they survived the later biome-identity restyle (`172c54857`) intact, as did the template structure in all six siblings.

## Applies when

- Creating or restyling any light theme in `shared/theme/builtInThemes/` (`type: "light"`), or building one via the `daintree-theme-creator` skill.
- Diagnosing "the light theme looks flat / washed out / gray / muddy" feedback that persists while all contrast and ramp audits pass.
- Tempted to express selection, hover, or an active tab as a darker fill or translucent dark wash on a near-white surface — check the elevation direction first.
- Adding or tuning audit gates in `shared/theme/oklch.ts` / `shared/theme/contrast.ts` — scope hard-fails by polarity and add the opposite-polarity guard test.
- Reading PR #9727 (or the #9711 thread) as a design reference — the merged file contradicts the PR narrative on the central composition decision.
